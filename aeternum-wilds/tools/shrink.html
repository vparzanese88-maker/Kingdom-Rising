Model Shrinker

Makes Meshy (or any) .glb models small enough to send. Most of a Meshy file is its huge 4K texture images; this shrinks those and keeps the model and animations exactly as they are. Everything happens on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

Drop your .glb files here, or click to choose themYou can drop many at once. Each one downloads as name-small.glb.
  1. In Meshy, download the model as GLB (not FBX/OBJ). If you got a .zip, unzip it and use the .glb inside.
  2. Drop it here and wait for the download.
  3. Send the -small.glb file in the chat.
