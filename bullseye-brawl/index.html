Bullseye
Brawl

A smoky bar. Two boards. One opponent standing right next to yours.
First to 300 points wins. Three darts a turn. Treble ring = triple, outer ring = double, bull 25, bullseye 50.
You stand beside your board and throw at the board across the bar, right where your opponent stands. Nothing stops you throwing at him instead, but darts won't kill him. Lean (A / D) or duck (C) when he lines one up on you.
Hold left click to wind up and let go to throw: let go on the green line and the dart lands on the crosshair, early drops low, late flies high. Shift holds your breath to steady your hand. You can hit anyone in the bar, your opponent too, but hit the same person twice and the police come and beat you to death with their clubs: you lose.
