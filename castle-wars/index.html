CASTLE WARS

Two castles. Two lords. Only one keep will stand.
You are the lord of the blue castle; the red castle faces you across the field. Before the battle you pick a class: Knight, Man-at-Arms, Pikeman, Spearman, Bowman, Crossbowman, Priest, Witch or Wizard. Waves of raiders hit both castles, and from round 2 the enemy lord marches on you. Kill enemies for gold, spend it on troops and defences, then break their gate, breach their walls and destroy their keep. You respawn at your keep while it stands.
