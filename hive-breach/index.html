The year is 2387. Alien swarms pour out of the Brood Queen's cave and march down the track toward your colony's power hub. Fight them yourself, build sentries in their path, and go into her cave and kill the Brood Queen to win. There are no waves: the swarm never stops, and the threat level rises every 60 seconds. The queen is guarded by Royal Guards and births more while you fight in her lair, so bring turrets, reinforcements and friends.

There is no money in this war: scrap does it all. Building a turret costs scrap (shown on its button) and a turret kit (you carry 2 and pick up more in the hub); welded upgrades burn scrap too. You start with 25 and find more in piles around wrecks. Every 60 seconds two supply crates parachute in (yellow beams): run over one for ammo, welder power and sometimes a special. A CPU commander sends reinforcements down the track and upgrades them as the threat level rises. Guns lose power with distance, so you need turrets. Aliens leave the track to hunt marines who come close, Juggernauts and Behemoths go after turrets, and every alien stops to tear down barricades.
