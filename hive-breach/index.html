Gold comes from kills, from surviving each wave, from supply pods (walk over the yellow beacons) and trickles in while you fight. Guns lose power with distance (a grey hit marker means a weak, long-range hit), so you need turrets. Aliens leave the track to hunt marines who come close (a red ! marks the ones after you), Brutes and Ravagers go off to smash turrets, and every alien stops to tear down barricades. Missiles, rockets, grenades and lightning pierce Brute armor. Mars has meteor showers, Kryos has blizzards and the jungle has alien nests that hatch ambushes: shoot them down. Wave 10 and 15 bring a Ravager; wave 20 brings the Hive Queen. The track climbs hills, crosses bridges over chasms and runs through tunnels: turrets cannot see aliens inside a tunnel, but you can wait at the exit, and anyone who falls into a chasm climbs out hurt.
