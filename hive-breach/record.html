OWFR TEAM FOXTROT · VOICE RECORDER

Record the commander, general and marine lines for the game. Marine lines are shouted out loud (no radio). Speak like you're on a battlefield radio: the game adds the radio crackle itself. Press Record, read the line, press Stop. Keyboard: Space starts and stops recording on the highlighted line, ↑ ↓ move between lines, P plays it back.

When you're done, press Download all. You get one zip: unzip it into hive-breach/assets/voice/ (or send it to Claude to add), and the game plays your voice whenever that line comes up.
