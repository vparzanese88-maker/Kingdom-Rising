Hold, Aim, Fire

You stand shoulder to shoulder with the Union line. Across 140 feet of open field, a hundred men in red march to meet you. Every second of the advance steadies every musket on the field, theirs and yours. Their officer will call his volley somewhere between 60 and 70 percent. Fire first and hit fewer, or wait and take his volley.
