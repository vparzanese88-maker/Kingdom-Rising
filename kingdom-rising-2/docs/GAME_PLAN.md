# Kingdom Rising II: the big picture

*A plan built from the owner's vision. It covers the story, systems and customisation, plus what's needed next.*

## The vision, in the owner's words

> Step one, defeat and gain a friend. Step two, defeat and gain a second friend. Step three, another friend, and prove
> your friendship and save your friends with love. Build a story, build a game, test the game, tweak it 100 times,
> then sell it to a huge audience. It's got to be a great story, fun, with customisation, and just plain fun to play.

The spine of the game is **friendship**. Every chapter ends with a new friend at your side. The finale is about
proving that friendship and saving the friends you made.

## The story spine: who are you?

You wash up on Goblos with **no memory**. That mystery carries the whole game:

- Each friend helps you win back a piece of your past. Who attacked your ship? Why were you at sea?
- The answer builds toward the finale. Whoever sank your ship comes for your friends, and you choose them over
  whatever you used to be.

## Chapters

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goblos** (built) | **Templeton**, a cute, crafty goblin shipwright | Defeat the Warchief's regime; Templeton sails with you | Musketeer, Bombardier, Blunderbuss | Rowboat to sailboat |

| 3 | *Proposal:* **the Thornwild**, a jungle island under a curse | *Proposal:* **Bramble**, a gentle troll druid twisted into the curse's guardian | You defeat the guardian, break the curse and free Bramble | Druid, Ranger, Beastmaster | Small ship to large ship |
| Finale | *Proposal:* the sea fortress of whoever sank your ship | All three | They take your friends; you go back for them. "Save your friends with love" | (all) | The large ship |

**Why "defeat and gain" works.** Templeton is gained through a shared enemy. Vex is gained by beating her and showing
mercy. Bramble is gained by defeating a curse, not the person. Each friendship begins differently, so the pattern
never feels repeated.

## What successful games teach us, applied here

- **Companions need their own goals, flaws and an arc that changes over time.** Just being in the party isn't
  enough. ([Game Developer: companions and player bonds](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/companions-creating-player-bonds),
  [Game Developer: balancing emotion and practicality](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/a-good-companion---balancing-emotions-and-practicality-when-designing-companion-characters),
  [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/dialogue-options-what-makes-a-good-video-game-companion))
  - Templeton's goal is to get off Goblos.
  - Vex's flaw is that she's only ever been paid to care.
  - Bramble's fear is hurting people again.
  - Friends should comment on places, argue a little, and grow closer.
- **Attachment comes from usefulness and from the risk of losing them.**
  ([Fistful of Wits](https://fistfulofwits.com/2013/10/19/emotional-attachment-in-games/),
  [Bouquet et al. 2018](https://www.medien.informatik.uni-muenchen.de/pubdb/publications/pub/bouquet2018mindtrek/bouquet2018mindtrek.pdf))
  - Every friend helps in play: Templeton builds traps and turrets, Vex fights, Bramble heals and brings beasts.
  - The finale puts them at real risk.
- **Start guided, then open up.**
  ([GamesRadar on Skyrim's opening](https://www.gamesradar.com/the-opening-hour-of-skyrim-is-still-the-best-thing-bethesda-has-ever-made),
  [The Outer Worlds 2 opening](https://gamedev.net/news/5749-open-vs-linear-setting-the-stakes-early-on-in-the-outer-worlds-2/))
  - Goblos now opens with a cinematic and safe first steps in Snagglefoot before the island opens.
  - Teach new mechanics in safe spaces, the way Breath of the Wild uses shrines.
- **The first hour must give mastery, purpose and immersion.**
  ([Chalmers thesis](https://odr.chalmers.se/server/api/core/bitstreams/c779f57d-f683-456d-bf63-62e812abb2d2/content),
  [Microsoft Research: the first hour](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/First20Hour20-20CHIPlay20201420-20preprint2.pdf))
  - Purpose: Templeton's boat.
  - Mastery: the first steps and the abilities that unlock at levels 1, 3, 6 and 9.
  - Immersion: the cinematic, the talking camera and a living village.

## Customisation (what makes players stay)

1. **Your hero.** Character creation with races and classes (proposal below).
2. **Your gear.** Swappable armour with the method on the Test Gear page. That's waiting on your decision after
   trying it.
3. **Your boat.** It grows every chapter: rowboat, sailboat, small ship, large ship. Later you could name it, paint
   it and fit cannons, and Templeton could build upgrades.
4. **Your home village.** Snagglefoot rebuilds as you help it, with new huts, a market and returning families. Seeing
   your effect on the world is what makes builder RPGs addictive.
5. **Your friends.** Each friend gets a small skill tree and outfits.

## Races and classes: salvage or new looks? (proposal)

**Recommendation: keep the classes' gameplay, refresh the looks.**

- The 14+ class kits already exist and are tuned: Warrior, Wizard, Rogue, Ranger, Berserker, Musketeer, Bombardier,
  Blunderbuss, Paladin, Cleric, Priest, Druid, Shaman, Monk, Necromancer, Bard and Elementalist. Each has abilities,
  animations, ultimates and effects. Throwing that away would cost months.
- The **looks** can be refreshed with Meshy to fit the island theme: sun-faded sailor colours, salvaged gear, goblin
  craftsmanship.
- **Races** come with the gear system. The Test Gear page proved one shared skeleton can carry Human, High Elf and
  Goblin bodies with swappable armour. Each class keeps its own weapons, and a race changes the body underneath.
  - **Starting races:** Human, High Elf, Goblin (all three bodies already exist).
  - **Unlockable later:** Dwarf (Gob's foundries), Orc (the finale's turncoats).
- **Class unlocks stay tied to islands,** as planned.

## Roadmap (suggested order)

1. **Playtest Goblos start to finish.** Tune fights, rewards and pacing; fix anything that feels slow or confusing.
2. **Give the champions and the Warchief real class kits.** The musketeer, bombardier and blunderbuss use their
   actual abilities, and the Warchief gets a two-phase fight.
3. **Templeton as a combat friend.** He follows you and builds a trap or turret on a cooldown.
4. **Rebuild Snagglefoot as you help.**
5. **Bring the gear system back** (if approved): loot and outfits on the Test Gear method.
6. **Character creation with races.**
7. **Chapter 2: Gob,** the port city, and Vex.
8. **Online co-op for the campaign.**
9. **Polish passes:** performance, sound, menus, a trailer and a store page.
