
## Weapons (third person)
New mechanism for every Kingdom Arena hero: the weapon is fixed in the hero model's closed fist (a per-hand "fist frame" worked out from the model's own hand skin: palm point toward the fingers, thumb side, finger direction), and the model's arms and hands follow the hidden built body's pose every frame (two-bone IK scaled to the model's arm, and the hand bone turned to the built hand's exact angle). So the weapon moves with the hand through idle, run, strafe, jump, dodge, attacks, abilities and the ultimate, with no angle copying onto the weapon itself (that caused the spinning). A support hand the built body has on the weapon (two-handers, the gun's fore-end) is put on the model's weapon itself. Shots: scratchpad/tp3/<class>_*.png (front row and side row).

## Balance fixes
From the balance tester's report (duels and Training Yard dummies, timers on game time). Duel checks below: 2 rounds a pair, bot vs bot, `?mode=arena&nomodels`.
- Bard and Beastmaster computer fighters "barely attack": they do attack (plain swings every ~1 s, Feral Roar, Crescendo, Mesmerize), but each blow was a third of everyone else's. Their class stat is dexterity while their weapons (sword, hand axes) run on strength, so their weapon damage came out at 370 and 315 against 770-1170 for the others (Druid 386 with a staff on intellect, Bombardier 295 the same). Fixed with ARENA_TUNE (game.js): bard dmg 1 -> 2.15 and hp 1 -> 1.1, beastmaster dmg 1 -> 2.05 and hp 1 -> 1.1 (the stat gap is about x2.1). In the bot: Pack Frenzy (buff `rampage`) was never cast (missing from the bot's damage-buff list) and now is, and it now quickens the bot's swings like it does for players. First check at x1.9/x1.8: bard damage per duel 180 -> 400-880, beastmaster 285 -> 190-710; still losing, hence the second step to x2.15/x2.05 and +10% health (not re-run: the machine's browser died).
- Necromancer Skull of Terror: not a bug. Training Yard check with timers on game time: the skull flies, lands and hits the dummy (664 at 8 m, 1,237 at 4 m). The 0 was the old harness. Druid Entangle: not a bug either. It is a 6 m ring round the druid, and the tester stood 10 m away: 454 at 4 m, 0 at 10 m.
- Ultimates cutting single-target damage: casting an ultimate holds the caster (`held`, no basic attacks) for its whole cinematic. Measured in the yard: Ranger 3.8 s, Paladin 4.5 s, Rogue 2.5 s, Shaman 2.5 s. Rain of Arrows: the ranger is now let go 1.3 s after the volley goes up (about 2.15 s in, was 3.85 s). Sticky Bombs use charges by design: the slot shows the number left (5) in its corner, and a cooldown only shows when all are gone (bomber.js bomberHud). Nothing changed.
