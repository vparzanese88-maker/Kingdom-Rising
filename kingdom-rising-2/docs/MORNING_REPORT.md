
## Weapons (third person)
New mechanism for every Kingdom Arena hero: the weapon is fixed in the hero model's closed fist (a per-hand "fist frame" worked out from the model's own hand skin: palm point toward the fingers, thumb side, finger direction), and the model's arms and hands follow the hidden built body's pose every frame (two-bone IK scaled to the model's arm, and the hand bone turned to the built hand's exact angle). So the weapon moves with the hand through idle, run, strafe, jump, dodge, attacks, abilities and the ultimate, with no angle copying onto the weapon itself (that caused the spinning). A support hand the built body has on the weapon (two-handers, the gun's fore-end) is put on the model's weapon itself. Shots: scratchpad/tp3/<class>_*.png (front row and side row).
- Warrior: unchanged look under the new mechanism (sword forward-down at rest, edge turned, shield face-out on the forearm); sword stays in the fist through every swing and the ultimate. tp3/warrior_v2.png
- Cleric: the winged hammer sits in her fist with its faces turned to strike forward (along the knuckles), held forward-down at rest and in hand through every swing, ability and the ultimate; shield face-out on the forearm. scratchpad/tp3/cleric_v2.png
- Shaman (new shaman_rig model): the new feathered spear (0.94 of her height) is gripped in her right fist, point up-forward at rest and on the run, point forward on the thrust; the doll rides in her left hand. scratchpad/tp3/shaman_v2.png
