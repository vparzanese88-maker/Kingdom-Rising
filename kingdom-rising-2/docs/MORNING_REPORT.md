
## Weapons (third person)
New mechanism for every Kingdom Arena hero: the weapon is fixed in the hero model's closed fist (a per-hand "fist frame" worked out from the model's own hand skin: palm point toward the fingers, thumb side, finger direction), and the model's arms and hands follow the hidden built body's pose every frame (two-bone IK scaled to the model's arm, and the hand bone turned to the built hand's exact angle). So the weapon moves with the hand through idle, run, strafe, jump, dodge, attacks, abilities and the ultimate, with no angle copying onto the weapon itself (that caused the spinning). A support hand the built body has on the weapon (two-handers, the gun's fore-end) is put on the model's weapon itself. Shots: scratchpad/tp3/<class>_*.png (front row and side row).
- Warrior: unchanged look under the new mechanism (sword forward-down at rest, edge turned, shield face-out on the forearm); sword stays in the fist through every swing and the ultimate. tp3/warrior_v2.png
- Cleric: the winged hammer sits in her fist with its faces turned to strike forward (along the knuckles), held forward-down at rest and in hand through every swing, ability and the ultimate; shield face-out on the forearm. scratchpad/tp3/cleric_v2.png
- Shaman (new shaman_rig model): the new feathered spear (0.94 of her height) is gripped in her right fist, point up-forward at rest and on the run, point forward on the thrust; the doll rides in her left hand. scratchpad/tp3/shaman_v2.png
- Rogue: a second dagger in her left fist (the arena gave her only one), so a detailed dagger sits in each closed fist, blades forward-down at rest and on the run, both moving with her hands through strafe, jump, every stab, abilities and the ultimate; no leftover built blades. Her stabs themselves are still small (an animation job). scratchpad/tp4/rogue_d2.png
- Beastmaster: the bear-claw gauntlets (models/w/beast_claw.fbx) are worn on both fists, pushed up the hand so the claws reach past the knuckles and fingertips, fur cuff over the wrist; they ride with his hands through idle, run, strafe, jump, both swipes, abilities and the ultimate; the old code claws and hand axe stay hidden. scratchpad/tp4/beastmaster_d2.png, beastmaster_c2.png

## Necromancer
Kit rework (Kingdom Arena and the Training Yard; code in src/necro.js, player.js reap(), arena.js bot logic):
- LMB, Scythe: a wide reaping sweep (act name `reap`, so the first-person view model plays its reap; third person and online show a turning two-handed swing). Reach = eye height x 1.35, capped 3.4 to 5 m (5 m at his current size); arc 132 degrees (1.15 rad either side); hits everyone in it; 0.62 x weapon damage per target, one swing per 0.89 s. Measured on a yard dummy: 1504 DPS at k 1.0 vs 942 DPS for the old poison ball (direct hit plus its poison), so k was set to 0.62 to give about the same single-target output. New reap whoosh and slice sounds (audio.js reap/reapHit), blood plus shadow hit burst, purple crescent. The green poison glow in his right hand is gone (third person; first person already had none).
- RMB: Bone Armor, unchanged.
- F, Life Siphon (replaces Soul Rend in the arena kit, same id `drainlife`): press to start, press again to stop; ends by itself after 5 s; 9 s cooldown from when it stops. Every 0.2 s the aim (crosshair, 20 m, walls block) looks for a player, computer fighter or mob: if one is there it takes 0.45 x weapon damage per second and slows; you heal 55% of it; purple beam plus green orbs and heal glow; the F slot glows while it is on. Verified: on target 2 s = 1518 damage and 373 healed; aimed away 1 s = 0 damage, 0 heal, beam hidden, still on; press again = off, cooldown 9; auto off at 5.02 s; dummy at 25 m = 0.
- Bots: melee with the scythe (range 4.6 m sweep) and start Life Siphon when facing their target within 17 m; they stop when they lose sight for 1.2 s. 150 s FFA bot test: 39 reaps, 9 siphons (21.9 s on).
- Online: damage goes through the normal hit messages; a new short 'siph' message draws the beam on other screens; `reap` is sent as swing2.

## Arena map (the Ruins of Aldermere)
Art pass on the Kingdom Arena map, layout and gameplay unchanged (same spawn rooms and gates, three routes, centre ring, balconies, stairs, ramps, zig-zag street walls, bot lanes). Art direction: docs/ARENA_ART.md. Code: src/ruinsmap.js (layout, now dressed) and the new src/ruinsart.js (textures, light, ground, Heartwood, backdrop, Meshy props); one line in src/main.js lets a map set its own light.
- Golden hour: a fixed low sun from the west-south-west (warm `#ffd09a`), cool blue sky bounce and fill, warm peach haze (fog 70 to 430 m), dusk-blue sky. Long shadows now cross the streets.
- Textures: cut-stone blocks, dark keep masonry, flagstones, cobbles, plaster with bare brick patches, planks, roof tiles, marble, bark, leaves, ivy and banner cloth, all drawn once on canvases at load (with normal maps) and mapped in world metres, so a long wall and a small block show the same stone size.
- Baked light (no per-frame cost): every piece gets a slight tint of its own, soot and shade at the foot of walls, moss on stone near the ground, and warm firelight from the 70-odd torches, braziers and lanterns (and Echo-blue or team-red from the crystals) baked into its vertices.
- The ground: one painted plane with a splat map: cobbled streets, a flagstone plaza and side approaches, grass and dirt paths in the garden, an earth outer path under the rampart, worn routes, weeds in the cracks, contact shadows at the foot of every wall and warm light pools under the fires. About 1,500 grass tufts in the garden and along the walls (one instanced mesh).
- The Heartwood (centre): a big twisted, buttressed trunk with roots breaking the paving, seven limbs, a crown of glowing gold leaves (the north-east side burned bare and black), hanging emberlight pods, and golden motes drifting up round it (animated in the shader). Its trunk now has a collider (1.7 m, up to 9.5 m), so arrows and line of sight stop at the trunk you can see. The ring round it is a new mosaic: rings of paving, a marble sunburst, root cracks.
- Braziers are real fires now: the game's four pooled fire lights and its fire particles follow the nearest ones.
- Pennant strings with lanterns over the high street, lily pads in the garden pond, ivy as leafy cut-out sheets instead of green slabs.
- The owner's Meshy tree and rock (models/props/yard_tree.fbx, yard_rock.fbx) replace the procedural trees and rocks once loaded (instanced), plus rocks along the outer walls (no shadows).
- Beyond the walls: broken towers, roofs and spires of the rest of Aldermere and low hills, hazy in the fog, on a warm plain (the campaign's green field no longer shows).
- Performance (same browser, same spot, 6v6 bots, measured back to back): scene triangles 833k before, 691k after; shadow-casting triangles 484k before, 368k after; draw calls for one frame 222 before, 246 after; one full render 12.5 s before vs 11.8 s after on this heavily loaded software-rendering machine (the same within noise). No console errors.
