
## Weapons (third person)
New mechanism for every Kingdom Arena hero: the weapon is fixed in the hero model's closed fist (a per-hand "fist frame" worked out from the model's own hand skin: palm point toward the fingers, thumb side, finger direction), and the model's arms and hands follow the hidden built body's pose every frame (two-bone IK scaled to the model's arm, and the hand bone turned to the built hand's exact angle). So the weapon moves with the hand through idle, run, strafe, jump, dodge, attacks, abilities and the ultimate, with no angle copying onto the weapon itself (that caused the spinning). A support hand the built body has on the weapon (two-handers, the gun's fore-end) is put on the model's weapon itself. Shots: scratchpad/tp3/<class>_*.png (front row and side row).
- Warrior: unchanged look under the new mechanism (sword forward-down at rest, edge turned, shield face-out on the forearm); sword stays in the fist through every swing and the ultimate. tp3/warrior_v2.png
- Cleric: the winged hammer sits in her fist with its faces turned to strike forward (along the knuckles), held forward-down at rest and in hand through every swing, ability and the ultimate; shield face-out on the forearm. scratchpad/tp3/cleric_v2.png
- Shaman (new shaman_rig model): the new feathered spear (0.94 of her height) is gripped in her right fist, point up-forward at rest and on the run, point forward on the thrust; the doll rides in her left hand. scratchpad/tp3/shaman_v2.png

## Necromancer
Kit rework (Kingdom Arena and the Training Yard; code in src/necro.js, player.js reap(), arena.js bot logic):
- LMB, Scythe: a wide reaping sweep (act name `reap`, so the first-person view model plays its reap; third person and online show a turning two-handed swing). Reach = eye height x 1.35, capped 3.4 to 5 m (5 m at his current size); arc 132 degrees (1.15 rad either side); hits everyone in it; 0.62 x weapon damage per target, one swing per 0.89 s. Measured on a yard dummy: 1504 DPS at k 1.0 vs 942 DPS for the old poison ball (direct hit plus its poison), so k was set to 0.62 to give about the same single-target output. New reap whoosh and slice sounds (audio.js reap/reapHit), blood plus shadow hit burst, purple crescent. The green poison glow in his right hand is gone (third person; first person already had none).
- RMB: Bone Armor, unchanged.
- F, Life Siphon (replaces Soul Rend in the arena kit, same id `drainlife`): press to start, press again to stop; ends by itself after 5 s; 9 s cooldown from when it stops. Every 0.2 s the aim (crosshair, 20 m, walls block) looks for a player, computer fighter or mob: if one is there it takes 0.45 x weapon damage per second and slows; you heal 55% of it; purple beam plus green orbs and heal glow; the F slot glows while it is on. Verified: on target 2 s = 1518 damage and 373 healed; aimed away 1 s = 0 damage, 0 heal, beam hidden, still on; press again = off, cooldown 9; auto off at 5.02 s; dummy at 25 m = 0.
- Bots: melee with the scythe (range 4.6 m sweep) and start Life Siphon when facing their target within 17 m; they stop when they lose sight for 1.2 s. 150 s FFA bot test: 39 reaps, 9 siphons (21.9 s on).
- Online: damage goes through the normal hit messages; a new short 'siph' message draws the beam on other screens; `reap` is sent as swing2.

## Goblin Bombardier
Finished the rework in src/bomber.js (keys: LMB bomb, Q sticky bombs, right click detonator, R Big Bertha; F Rocket Boots and C Smoke Bomb unchanged):
- LMB: throws his own hand bomb model (makeBomb + the bomb_grenade kit model, like the one in his fist), in an arc; it now always bounces 3 times when it lands in the open (restitution 0.45, each hop lower), then goes off 0.45 s after it settles (3.2 s at most; at once on hitting an enemy). Verified at four pitches: 3 bounces each, explosion at 1.7 to 3.2 s.
- Q sticky bombs: 5 charges; the recharge (1 every 0.5 s) now starts only once all 5 are thrown. Verified: 4,3,2,1,0 while throwing, then 1,1,2,2,3,3,4 every 0.25 s. Max 5 out: a 6th removes the oldest (ids 12-16 -> 15-19). Stick to the ground, walls and enemies and move with them (dummy moved 3 m, bomb offset unchanged).
- Right click detonator: sets off every thrown and sticky bomb at once; detonated stickies burst 4.5 m (instead of 3.2 m) at full damage (vs 0.6 when one times out after 20 s): 1427 -> 2591 on a dummy at 1 m; at 4 m 0 -> 1652.
- R Big Bertha: 0.8 s plant at your feet, no cinematic or third-person camera; 9 s fuse with spatialised beeps (louder nearer, 1.05 s apart down to 0.1 s); 16 m radius (dummy at 12 m hit, at 17.4 m not); the detonator does not touch it. Enemies can still defuse it by standing on it 3 s.
- Bots: 150 s FFA test: 46 bombs, 100 sticky bombs (up to 3 stuck on fighters at once), 1 Big Bertha.
- Paladin: no paladin greatsword among the owner's uploads (all 22 weapon uploads are already in models/w), so he keeps the built greatsword, now with a thicker blade (no more paper-thin dashed line edge-on), 0.69 of his height, both hands on the grip, tip tilted forward at rest instead of straight up.
