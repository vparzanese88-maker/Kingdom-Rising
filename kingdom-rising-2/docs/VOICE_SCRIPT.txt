# KINGDOM ARENA: FULL VOICE SCRIPT

## TIPS (read these first)
1. Record in a quiet room, with your mouth about one hand-width from the mic. A closet or a blanket over your head stops echo.
2. Before each class, say the class name out loud ("Paladin").
3. Say every line 3 TIMES: calm, normal, then BIG. I keep the best one.
4. Pause 2 SECONDS after every line. That's how I cut them apart.
5. If you mess up, say "again" and redo the line. I'll throw out the bad take.
6. Follow the [direction] in brackets: it tells you how to say the line.
7. Over-act it. Game voices are bigger than real life. When it says SHOUT, really shout.
8. At the end of each class, record 3 pain grunts ("ugh!" "argh!" "hnn!"), 2 attack yells ("hyah!" "raa!") and 1 death cry.
9. Drink some water between classes. Shouting dries your throat.
10. You can record it all in one file or split it into several. Send m4a, mp3 or wav.
11. Don't worry about your voice: I change each class's voice afterwards (deeper, demon, goblin, holy echo and so on).
