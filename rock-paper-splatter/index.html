    Rock Paper Splatter

    Six chairs. One table. Losers go out in pieces.

    Up to 6 players sit round the table. Everyone picks rock, paper or scissors at once. If all three show, or everyone matches, it's a tie and you go again. Otherwise whoever threw the losing sign explodes. Last one alive takes the pot.
    Online: one player hosts and reads out the 4-letter code, friends type it and press Join. The host can fill empty chairs with CPU players.
